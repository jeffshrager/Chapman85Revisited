## Proved (ground tier: no variables)
- Truth criterion for complete plans (`mem_run_iff`); time's arrow (`run_append`).
- Completions. One lemma does the work of Chapman's temporal consistency lemma and all his
  falsifying constructions: any ranking monotone in the order has a completion sorted by rank
  (`exists_completion_rank`). Gives the completion lemma and `necBefore_iff`.
- **The modal truth criterion, both directions** (`mtc_iff`). Chapman's proof goes through
  nearly word for word; the white-knight regress becomes an induction from the right
  (`mem_run_of_covered`).
- Correctness of TWEAK's termination test (`correctness`).
- Breakfast plan (p. 7) and the Sussman trace (pp. 14–17) checked by `decide` via the criterion.
- Intractability reduction correct (`Tweak.Extended.reduction_correct`), with
  situation-dependent effects (`xrun`). Plans were generalized from `Fin n` to any enumerable
  step type (`Enum`) to make this proof readable.
- McAllester's synergy example (`Tweak.Extended.Synergy`).

## What came out differently from the report
1. **Possible truth is not "exactly analogous with the modalities switched"** (p. 9): the
   switched criterion is necessary (`switched_of_possTrue`) but not sufficient
   (`switched_not_sufficient`, a five-step plan).
2. **White knights are needed without variables** (`wk_needed`); the report motivates the
   clause only by the odd plan, which needs variables.
3. **The looping example of the outcomes lemma (p. 44) fails rather than loops.** It is
   unsolvable (`loop_unsolvable`), but no operator re-asserts ¬g or ¬h (`loop_no_reassert`), so
   TWEAK exhausts the space after 9 plans. Moving the conditions into postconditions
   (g,¬h / h,¬g) gives the described loop: unsolvable (`loop2_unsolvable`), search grows
   5 → 17 → 93 → 813.
4. **Intractability is coNP-hardness** (reduction from DNF validity); NP-hard only under Turing
   reductions.
